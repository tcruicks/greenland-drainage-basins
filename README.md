Greenland is under warming pressure and surface melting is increasing due to climate change.  Increasing melting produces larger volumes of cold fresh water that enter the surrounding oceans and have impacts on ocean currents.  This project looks at the ice sheet's major drainage basins and what the observational records suggests about air temperature warming and how well the climate reanalysis ERA5 matches the observational record.  I am redoing this project as a re-learning exercise after three years away from python.
